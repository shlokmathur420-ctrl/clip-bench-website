Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Clip Bench is a personal-use application. Its processing workflow runs locally on the computer where it is installed. When you choose to publish content, the relevant media and information are sent to the platform you select.

1. Information processed

Clip Bench may process video URLs, downloaded video and audio files, transcripts, subtitles, generated titles and descriptions, hashtags, clip files, and publishing settings. This information is used to provide the application's clipping and publishing features.

2. Local processing and storage

Video downloading, transcription, clip rendering and local metadata generation are designed to run on your Mac using locally installed tools. Project files, generated clips, configuration files and authentication tokens may be stored on that computer. Their exact storage and retention depend on the application's configuration and your actions.

3. YouTube and Google services

If you connect a Google or YouTube account, Clip Bench uses Google authorization to access the permissions you approve. These permissions are used for the YouTube features you choose, such as uploading videos. When you publish, the selected video and associated metadata are sent to YouTube and handled under Google's applicable terms and policies.

Google account authorization data, including tokens, may be stored locally so the application can maintain its connection. Do not share these files or publish them in a public code repository.

4. Instagram services

If you connect an Instagram account, Clip Bench uses the applicable Meta services and permissions to support the Instagram publishing features you choose. Publishing a Reel sends the selected video and caption to the relevant Meta/Instagram services.

5. Third-party services

Clip Bench may interact with third-party services such as YouTube, Google, Instagram, Meta and video-hosting or delivery services needed for publishing. Those services process information under their own privacy policies and terms. Source video providers may also receive requests when a video is downloaded.

6. Sharing and publishing

Clip Bench does not operate its own cloud video-processing service. However, information may leave your computer when you use connected services, create a public delivery link for a video, or upload or schedule content. Published content is subject to the visibility settings and policies of the destination platform.

7. Data retention and security

Local project files, media and authentication tokens may remain on your computer until you delete them. You are responsible for protecting your device, keeping credentials private and removing files you no longer need. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

8. Your choices

You can disconnect connected accounts, revoke permissions through the relevant platform's account settings, and delete locally stored project files and tokens. Deleting local files does not automatically delete content already uploaded to YouTube or Instagram.

9. Children's privacy

Clip Bench is not designed to collect personal information from children. It is intended for personal use by its operator.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the application's features or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.

11. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the application operator through the contact information provided directly to authorized users.
